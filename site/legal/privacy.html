Fulton Command Center — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026 · Version 1.0

Fulton Command Center is an internal software application operated by WEF Management LLC, a California limited liability company, doing business as Fulton Management ("Fulton Management"), a U.S. accounting and bookkeeping firm. It is used only by Fulton Management employees and authorized contractors to perform bookkeeping and accounting services for Fulton Management's clients under existing client engagement agreements. It is not a consumer product, and it is not offered to the public. This policy explains what data the application accesses, why, how it is protected, and who to contact with questions.

1. What Data the Application Accesses

With each client's authorization (via Intuit's OAuth consent flow), Fulton Command Center connects to that client's QuickBooks Online company and accesses:

  • Financial records — chart of accounts, general ledger and transaction data, vendor and customer lists, account balances, and financial reports;
  • Source documents — invoices, receipts, bills, deposit backup, and similar documents provided by clients or attached to transactions, which the application may also upload and attach to QuickBooks transactions;
  • Connection and usage metadata — OAuth tokens, connection status, and internal records of which Fulton Management staff member reviewed or approved which work.

The application does not access client data beyond what is needed for the bookkeeping services described below, and it accesses QuickBooks Online only through Intuit's authorized APIs.

2. Why the Data Is Accessed

Data is accessed solely to deliver the bookkeeping and accounting services each client has already engaged Fulton Management to perform: categorizing and recording transactions, attaching source documentation, monitoring the health and reconciliation status of client books, and running accuracy checks on posted work. The application does not use client data for advertising, and it does not use client data for any purpose outside the client's engagement with Fulton Management.

3. How Data Is Stored and Protected

  • Encryption: data is encrypted at rest, and connections use HTTPS/TLS in transit. QuickBooks OAuth tokens are additionally encrypted at the application layer (AES-256-GCM) with versioned keys.
  • Hosting: the application and its database are hosted on U.S.-based cloud infrastructure (Railway).
  • Access control: access is limited to Fulton Management staff and authorized contractors, authenticated through single sign-on restricted to Fulton Management-managed accounts. Client companies do not share data with one another, and staff access is limited to legitimate work needs.
  • Human review: transactions created by the application are subject to human review workflows and independent after-the-fact accuracy checks.

4. Automated Processing and Artificial Intelligence

Fulton Command Center proposes transaction categorizations, computes deposit and journal-entry splits, and flags possible errors using rules derived from each client's own historical QuickBooks data. This processing runs entirely on infrastructure operated by Fulton Management. Client data is not transmitted to any third-party artificial-intelligence provider, and no client data is used to train any third-party model.

All proposals are suggestions only. Fulton Management staff review and approve work product before it is recorded, the application records who approved what, and any entry it writes to QuickBooks Online can be reversed.

If Fulton Management later introduces third-party AI processing — for example, to read information from source documents — it will do so only under a data-processing agreement that prohibits retention of client data and prohibits its use for model training, and this policy will be updated before any such processing begins.

5. Service Providers

Fulton Management uses a limited set of service providers to operate the application — cloud hosting and database services (Railway) and standard operational tooling (such as error monitoring and uptime alerting). Service providers process data only to provide their services to Fulton Management and are bound by contractual confidentiality and data-protection obligations.

6. No Sale of Data

Fulton Management does not sell, rent, or trade client data or any personal information accessed through Fulton Command Center, and does not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

7. Retention

Client data and source documents are retained for as long as needed to perform the client's engagement and to meet Fulton Management's legal, tax, and professional recordkeeping obligations. When a client engagement ends, data is retained, returned, or deleted in accordance with the engagement agreement and applicable recordkeeping requirements. Clients may revoke the QuickBooks Online connection at any time through Intuit's app-management settings, which stops further data access by the application.

8. Your Choices and Questions

Clients with questions about how their data is handled — or who wish to review, correct, or request deletion of data held by Fulton Management — should contact Fulton Management using the details below or through their engagement contact. Fulton Management staff with questions about internal data handling should contact management.

9. Changes to This Policy

Fulton Management may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available at this address, with its effective date shown above. Material changes affecting client data handling will be communicated to affected clients.
